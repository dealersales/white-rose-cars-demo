Apply for Finance

Hire Purchase (HP)

Hire Purchase is a straightforward way to buy a car. You pay a deposit, then make fixed monthly payments over an agreed term. Once all payments are made, the car is yours.

  • Own the car at the end
  • Fixed monthly payments
  • No mileage restrictions

Personal Contract Purchase (PCP)

PCP offers lower monthly payments with flexibility at the end of the agreement. You pay a deposit, make monthly payments, then choose to return the car, pay the optional final payment, or part-exchange.

  • Lower monthly payments
  • Flexibility at the end
  • Easy to upgrade regularly

Finance options and the terms that apply to your circumstances are discussed with you before you commit to anything. Ask us for a personalised quotation.

Written quotations are available on request.

Car Finance FAQs

Straight answers on car finance, credit applications, deposits and early settlement.

We work with a range of finance providers, some of whom may be able to help if you have a limited or imperfect credit history. Every application is assessed on your individual circumstances. Get in touch for a no-obligation chat.

Car finance lets you spread the cost of a vehicle over time through monthly payments instead of paying the full amount upfront. Once you have found the right car on our stock page, we can discuss the options available to you.

A deposit reduces the amount you borrow, which can lower your monthly payments. The required deposit varies by vehicle and finance provider, but many customers put down around 10% or more. We will confirm the exact figure with your application.

Many finance agreements allow you to settle early, often subject to an early-settlement fee. This can save you interest. Speak to the finance provider to check the terms of your specific agreement.

A soft-search eligibility check generally does not affect your credit score. Some providers may run a hard search before approving an agreement, which does count. We are always transparent about the checks required.

Cookie Preferences

Cookies are small data files that websites store on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit them. They act like tiny pieces of information websites remember about you. Here are some key things to know about cookies:

  • They make websites work better: Cookies can help websites remember things you've done on previous visits.
  • They personalise your experience: Cookies can be used to show you content relevant to your interests and browsing habits.
  • They provide website analytics: Websites can use cookies to understand how visitors use their site, helping them improve your experience.
It's important to note:

Cookies typically don't store personal information that identifies you directly (like your name or email address).
