More about this SEAT Leon

Very low mileage! The 2019 SEAT Leon TDI SE Dynamic is a practical hatchback that combines efficiency with everyday usability. Powered by a 1.6 litre diesel engine, this vehicle meets Euro 6 emission standards, making it a sensible choice for modern driving requirements. With a recorded mileage of 28,480 miles, this example has covered a relatively low distance for its age. Catergory N hence price. \n\nThis model offers several features that enhance the driving experience, including SEAT Full Link smartphone integration, which supports Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, and Mirror Link for seamless connectivity. Parking is made straightforward with the inclusion of both front and rear parking sensors, while the air conditioning system ensures comfort for all passengers. Beyond its technology, the vehicle is notable for its large boot space, which provides ample room for luggage or shopping. Cookie Preferences

Cookies are small data files that websites store on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit them. They act like tiny pieces of information websites remember about you. Here are some key things to know about cookies:

  • They make websites work better: Cookies can help websites remember things you've done on previous visits.
  • They personalise your experience: Cookies can be used to show you content relevant to your interests and browsing habits.
  • They provide website analytics: Websites can use cookies to understand how visitors use their site, helping them improve your experience.
It's important to note:

Cookies typically don't store personal information that identifies you directly (like your name or email address).
