# White Rose Cars — demo preview

A static preview of a redesigned website for **White Rose Cars**, Unit 4, Sancorp Business Park,
17 Larchfield Road, Leeds LS10 1QP.

**This is a design preview, not the live shopfront.** It is noindexed, carries a demo badge, and must never be
pointed at a client domain or used to take real enquiries.

Live preview: https://dealersales.github.io/white-rose-cars-demo/

## What is real, and what is not

- **Real:** the dealer's own stock — 7 cars, with their published prices, mileages, specifications,
  registration marks and photographs, taken from whiterosecarsales.co.uk. Descriptions are the dealer's own
  words, trimmed before their finance line.
- **Real:** the servicing copy. The ten service pages are adapted from the service documents Jon Ireland
  supplied on 18 November 2026, keeping his wording; the wet belt page is ours (his set did not include one).
- **Real:** the phone number, the email address and the address, which is now Larchfield Road — the business
  has moved from 408 Oakwood Lane.
- **Not real:** the design, the copy around the cars, and the photography treatment.
- **Not included:** finance rates or a representative example, reviews, a live Carro stock feed, a
  CodeWeavers finance widget, any company or FCA number, and any servicing price — the £79/£99 starting price
  is still to be confirmed by the dealer, so the offer card asks for the price instead of stating one.
- **Missing photographs:** the Toyota RAV4 has none on the dealer's own site, so it shows the placeholder.

Built on the Digital Sales Consultants dealer template. Enquiries from this preview go nowhere.
