# White Rose Cars — demo preview

A static preview of a redesigned website for **White Rose Cars**, 408 Oakwood Lane, Roundhay, Leeds LS8 3LG.

**This is a design preview, not the live shopfront.** It is indexed against noindex, carries a demo badge,
and must never be pointed at a client domain or used to take real enquiries.

Live preview: https://dealersales.github.io/white-rose-cars-demo/

## What is real, and what is not

- **Real:** the dealer's own stock — 7 cars, with their published prices, mileages, specifications,
  registration marks and photographs, taken from whiterosecarsales.co.uk on 10 November 2026. Descriptions
  are the dealer's own words, trimmed before their finance line.
- **Real:** the phone number, the email address and the trading address, as published on their site.
- **Not real:** the design, the copy around the cars, the photography treatment, and the "demo preview"
  badge. Opening hours are appointment-only by their own statement, so no fixed times are claimed anywhere.
- **Not included:** finance rates or a representative example (no APR has been supplied), reviews (they have
  none published on Google that we can verify), a Carro stock feed, a CodeWeavers finance widget, and any
  company or FCA number.
- **Missing photographs:** the Toyota RAV4 has none on the dealer's own site, so it shows the placeholder.

Built on the Digital Sales Consultants dealer template. Enquiries from this preview go nowhere.
